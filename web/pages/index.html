PvP first-person MOBA shooter · pre-alpha

Hold the lane.
Break the Uplink.

Cybergram is five against five on three lanes, played in first person. Pick one of seven heroes, fight beside your Wardling squad, capture hardpoints to push the front, and take down the enemy Mana Uplink. Aim decides the duel, the team decides the match.

Three lanes, one front

Hardpoints move the front line. Hold them with your team and your Wardlings to open the way to the enemy base.

Heroes with a kit

Every hero has a weapon, four abilities and an ultimate. Level up during the match and build your kit at the Armory.
