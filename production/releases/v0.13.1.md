# Cybergram v0.13.1: The new HUD (pre-alpha)

The network protocol is unchanged (17), so v0.13.0 players and v0.13.1
servers work together. The launcher stays at 1.3.0.

## For server hosts

The v0.13.0 server image was never published, because a build check still
expected the old single-process server. **Use the v0.13.1 image.** It
contains everything from v0.13.0, and the launcher update feed for v0.13
comes with it. The steps in `docs/HOSTING.md` ("Upgrading to v0.13") are
unchanged.

## A new HUD

The whole in-match HUD has been redesigned in the style of the menus: ink
panels, brass accents and the menu fonts.

- **Top centre:**
  - match clock
  - framed Uplink bars for both teams
  - the three lanes on one line under them
  - Overtime and Sudden Death states
- **Bottom:**
  - Health and shields on the left, with your squad above them.
  - Your skills in the middle: each of the 28 skills has its own icon. The
    key shown under each skill is your current binding, or the gamepad
    button when you play with a pad.
  - Your weapon and ammo on the right.
- **Around the screen:** a framed minimap, the kill feed, an objective card
  that tells you what to do next, and short pop-up notices.
- **Screens:**
  - **Death screen:** the world turns grey and shows who killed you.
  - **Scoreboard (Tab) and Armory:** the game behind them is blurred.
  - **End of match:** a new banner.
- **Armory:** new line-art icons for every item.

## New HUD settings (Gameplay tab)

- **Fade when idle:** the HUD fades out after 4 or 8 seconds of calm, or
  stays on with Off. It comes back at once when you shoot, cast, take damage,
  score a kill, or an objective changes. The default is 4 seconds.
- **HUD text size:** 90–130%.

## Still the same

Colour-blind presets (F6), reduce motion, screen effects intensity, the
dynamic crosshair and the comfort options all work with the new HUD.
Information shown by team colour also has a shape, so it doesn't rely on
colour alone.

## Known gaps

- **Scoreboard:** shows kills and deaths only. Assists and respawn timers
  aren't sent to the HUD yet.
- **Death screen:** names the hero that killed you, but not the ability.
- **Sudden Death:** the squad strip and objective card stay on screen.
- **End of match:** the match HUD stays visible behind the end banner.
- **Small screens:** at 1280×720 only some HUD states have been checked
  closely.
