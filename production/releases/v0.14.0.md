# Cybergram v0.14.0: New heroes up close (pre-alpha)

The network protocol is unchanged (17), so v0.13 servers and players still
work together. The launcher updates itself to 1.4.0 for the new hero
portraits.

## All seven heroes rebuilt

Every hero has a new, detailed body in the Cybergram comic style, with
painted wear and cloth that moves.

- **Vesper:** a sculpted mask with a hinged jaw, a gold filigree crest, gold
  kintsugi cracks and violet tear streaks.
- **Ryker:** a visor helmet with glowing lenses, a rebreather and a plate
  carrier, with his own AR-7.
- **Brannoc:** a closed helm with a glowing slit, layered shoulder plates and
  gauntlets, with his own Ironmaw. He now holds it at chest height.
- **Hex:** a hood over a white helmet whose screen face shows a pixel smiley.
- **Liora:** a medic helmet, halo drones and a layered coat.
- **Sable:** a steel slit mask with jade claw marks, and a hood with a scarf.
- **Juniper:** brass goggles, a rebreather and a big tool-pack.

Each hero also has their own idle animation and a new launcher portrait.

## Your own weapon and hands in first person

- **Your hero's weapon:** you now see your hero's own weapon, with gloved
  hands that have real fingers and your hero's sleeves.
- **Animations:** each hero has 12 first-person animations, including draw,
  fire, reload, cast, ultimate, sprint and inspect.
- **Reload timing:** the reload lasts exactly as long as your weapon's reload
  time.
- **Inspect:** a new key, **I** by default (Settings > Controls), shows off
  your weapon.
- **Death and respawn:** you holster on death and draw again on respawn.
- **Comfort settings:** these all still apply: field-of-view fitting, weapon
  bob off, and reduce motion.

## HUD polish

- **Key prompts follow your bindings:** the Armory prompt and the reload hint
  show the key you bound, or the gamepad button. The Armory now opens with
  your Interact key instead of always F.
- **Gamepad:** the Fork choice shows the pad buttons that actually pick it
  (LB / RB).
- **"Not ready" feedback:** pressing a skill that is on cooldown or locked
  flashes the slot and says why.
- **Squad state:** the squad line says RETURNING or DISSOLVING when that is
  what your Wardlings are doing.
- **Sudden Death and match end:** Sudden Death hides the squad strip and
  objective card. The end screen now shows only the result.
- **Skill points:** "+N SP" shows the key that spends them (Alt by default).
- **1280×720:** the Lumen gain no longer overlaps the resonance number.

## Known gaps

- **Death screen at 1280×720:** the skill-points line overlaps the skill
  bar labels.
- **First-person framing:** Brannoc's drum gun takes up a large part of the
  lower screen while he reloads.
- **First-person tint:** the Chamber mount colour doesn't show on the
  first-person weapon yet.
- **Sable:** her mantle is shorter than planned.
- **Not yet checked on real graphics hardware:** frame rates with the new
  heroes and first-person models.
