# Cybergram v0.10.0: Shardline Front (pre-alpha)

The network protocol is unchanged (14), so v0.9.0 players and servers still
work together. Matches on the new map need both sides on 0.10.0. Updating is
recommended.

## Shardline Front: the full 3-lane map

The game now plays on its real map, a 5v5 front with three lanes.

- **Three lanes, five hardpoints each:**
  - North is a wide bridge lane, Center runs through a market, and South
    runs along the docks.
  - Each lane has Inner and Outer hardpoints per side and a Mid in the middle.
    Every hardpoint is one of three task types: Breach, Hold or Plant.
- **Flanks:** eight Undercroft tunnels link each Outer to the next lane's Mid,
  for routes around a stalled lane.
- **Mid Plaza:** a round plaza in the centre joins the three Mids.
- **Sudden Death** (new): if the clock runs out with no winner, the fight
  moves to the Mid Plaza.
  - A ring shrinks over 90 seconds, and anyone outside it takes damage.
  - There are no respawns; the last team standing wins.
- **All three lanes work in play:**
  - Each lane sends its own waves.
  - The Forward Beacon spawns you at the Mid of the lane you died in.
  - Go Capture works on any hardpoint.
- **Bots:** they open on a 2/2/1 lane split and move to whichever lane needs
  help most.
- **HUD:**
  - a lane minimap in the top left
  - a three-row front strip showing who holds each hardpoint
  - a lane summary on the scoreboard
- **Modes:**
  - Online and Vs Bots are now 5v5 on Shardline Front.
  - The old one-lane map stays as **Quick Match** (3v3), a shorter mode.

## HD heroes

All seven heroes are rebuilt in more detail, in the same toon style.

- **More detail on the models:**
  - belts with buckles, pouches, holsters and bandoliers
  - riveted bracers, collars, back power cells with cables, helmet vents
  - weapons gain rails, sights, side plates and glowing team strips
- **Texture maps:** each hero now has its own painted colour, normal and
  detail maps. They add panel lines, rivets, seams, cloth weave and worn
  metal edges, and metal parts catch a sharp highlight.
- **Things that swing:** Vesper's coat tails, Sable's scarf, Liora's halo,
  Hex's antennae, Ryker's antenna and Juniper's backpack now move with the
  body.
- **Animation:** skill casts have more snap and follow-through, and shooting
  has a kick. Heroes on the home screen play a new idle pose.
- **Performance:** a lighter model replaces each hero beyond 25 m.
- **Portraits** are re-rendered from the new models.

## One installer per platform

The game and launcher now come in one installer, and the launcher is what you
start. It keeps the game up to date.

- **Windows:** `CybergramSetup-0.10.0.exe`.
  - Installs for your user only, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Cybergram`, with no
    admin prompt.
  - Adds a Start menu entry and an optional desktop shortcut, and appears in
    Add/Remove Programs.
  - Uninstalling asks whether to also delete your settings.
- **Linux:**
  - `Cybergram-0.10.0-x86_64.AppImage`: make it executable and run it.
  - Or `CybergramInstaller-0.10.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`: unpack it and run
    `install.sh`, which adds a menu entry. Remove it with `uninstall.sh`.
- **Portable builds:** the zip and tar.gz files are still available for
  advanced users.
- **Help:** `docs/INSTALL.md` covers locations, uninstalling and checksums.

## Known gaps

- **Map:**
  - The flank tunnels are shorter than designed (about 75 m instead of 110 m).
  - The North and South roads out of each base are flat rather than ramped.
  - Garrisons, supply caches and barricades are placed on the map, but they
    don't work in play yet.
  - Dock water doesn't slow you down yet.
  - Sudden Death has no on-screen ring yet; the phase is shown as text.
  - Full-length bot matches end with few captures, so match length and
    balance on the full map still need playtesting.
- **Installers:**
  - The Windows installer may not be code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn
    "Unknown publisher". Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  - The AppImage updates the game but not itself. When a new launcher is out,
    it tells you to download the new AppImage.
  - The Windows installer has only been build-checked, not yet run on Windows.
- **Heroes:**
  - Body shapes are unchanged: there is no real layered or simulated cloth,
    and Liora's skirt still shows a dark patch.
  - Some armour still looks a little blotchy.
  - The energy weapons (Vesper, Liora, Hex) gained less detail than the
    rest.
  - The new backward-falling death is in the models but isn't used yet.
  - Heroes cost about 1.5 times more to draw than in 0.9 (measured on
    software rendering only).
- **Launcher text** is still English-only.
