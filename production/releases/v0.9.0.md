# Cybergram v0.9.0: new look and toon heroes (pre-alpha)

The network protocol is unchanged (14), so v0.8.0 players and servers still
work together. Updating is recommended for the new look.

## A new look for the client, lobby and launcher

The menus are rebuilt around one premium-dark style. It replaces the boxed,
glowing purple look from v0.8.

- **Colours and type:**
  - deep ink background
  - brass as the single action colour
  - teal only for status (online, picking)
- **Fonts:** Chakra Petch for headings, IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono for text
  and numbers (all under the SIL Open Font License).
- **Fewer boxes:** content sits on the background, separated by thin lines and
  space. Main buttons are brass with cut corners, and skills show as key chips
  (Q / E / C / G).
- **Real hero portraits** replace the coloured hexagons with initials:
  - in the friends list
  - in the team slots, the hero picker and your account avatar
  - centred on each hero's mask
- **Main menu:**
  - A large hero with its role, weapon, a one-line description and its skills.
  - **PLAY** opens a mode list, and **HEROES** shows the full roster.
  - The friends rail reveals Join / Accept / Block when you hover a friend.
- **Lobby:** full screen.
  - Teams sit on the left and right as portrait rings: teal for picking, brass
    for locked.
  - Your hero stands in the centre under a spotlight, with abilities on the
    right.
  - Chat at the bottom left, and a brass **LOCK IN** button.
- **Launcher:**
  - An icon rail and three heroes as key art.
  - Patch headline and news list.
  - A brass **UPDATE / PLAY** button that fills with progress.

## All seven heroes rebuilt in a toon style

The block figures are gone. Every hero is now a rigged, animated character in
a cel-shaded, comic-ink style:

- **Masks and helmets, no faces:**
  - Ryker: tactical visor
  - Vesper: porcelain marionette mask
  - Brannoc: closed helm
  - Liora: medic visor and halo
  - Sable: hooded slit mask
  - Juniper: goggles and rebreather
  - Hex: hood with a screen face
- **Shading:** thick ink outlines, hard two-tone cel shading, light hatching in
  the shadows, and bold colours. Your team colour shows as an accent stripe and
  outline.
- **Motion capture** for walking, running, jumping and dying, recorded from
  real actors (CMU Graphics Lab database). Hand-made animation is layered on
  top for aiming, shooting, reloading, skill casts and the idle pose.
- Other players' heroes now visibly shoot, cast and fall when they die.

## Known gaps

- **Animations:**
  - Strafing and crouching are hand-animated rather than motion capture.
  - Other players' reloads aren't shown, because reloads aren't sent over the
    network yet.
- **Visual details:**
  - Liora's coat is a simple cone.
  - Brannoc holds his weapon low.
  - Sable is very dark by design.
- **Performance:** the outlines cost some speed on very weak (software)
  graphics. It hasn't been measured on a real graphics card yet.
- **Launcher text** is still English-only.
