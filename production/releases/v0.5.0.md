# Cybergram v0.5.0: all heroes, shop, settings, launcher, accounts (pre-alpha)

## Heroes: all seven playable

- **Ryker Vance** (Breakline AR-7):
  - Frag Grenade, 2 charges.
  - Combat Stim: +25 % fire rate and +15 % speed, costs 20 HP.
  - Tactical Slide: reloads 30 % of the magazine.
  - Ultimate, Overdrive Protocol: bottomless magazine and +30 % damage.
  - Passive, Battle Rhythm: a kill refills half the magazine.
- **Liora Vale** (Halo Repeater), support:
  - Heal beam on the right mouse button, rebindable.
  - Med-Pack Drone and Prism Ward shield.
  - Flash Bloom: blinds and knocks back.
  - Ultimate, Aurora: healing dome.
  - Passive, Triage Kit: free Med-Packs.
- **Sable** (Whisperfang SMG), assassin:
  - Veilwalk: stealth.
  - Phase Shift: invulnerable dash.
  - Sabotage Charge: remote mine.
  - Ultimate, Eclipse Step: silences and teleports behind the target.
  - Passive, Shadowgraph: bonus damage from behind.
- **Juniper Quill:** a trapper kit with charge-based skills (tripwires, zones, Relay Hop).
- **Hex:** a hacker kit with hacks and Zero-Day. Signal Sight +50 % also hits Wardlings.
- **Bots** play the new heroes, with a few exceptions:
  - no heal beam
  - no Sabotage remote detonation
  - no Tripwire or Relay Hop

## Shop

- A full-screen shop in the League of Legends style. Open it with **B** on the
  shop pad; a hint shows when you're off it.
  - Categories, item icons, and a recommended build for each hero.
  - Undo gives a full refund while you're still in the shop. Selling refunds 60 %.

## Settings

- The settings menu has four tabs: Video, Audio, Controls and Gameplay.
- **Video:** a graphics quality setting (Low / Medium / High / Ultra).
- **Audio:** separate Effects and UI volumes.
- **Controls:** every keyboard and mouse action can be rebound (24 actions).
  - Conflicts swap automatically.
  - "Reset to defaults" restores the original keys.
- **Gameplay:** crosshair options.

## Graphics

- New sky, sea and city skyline, lit environment panels, and better lighting and fog.
- Effects for muzzle flashes, hits and kills on top of the tracers.
- Quality tiers scale shadows, ambient occlusion and effects.

## Launcher

- **CybergramLauncher** (Windows / Linux zip below) checks the official server
  for a new version, downloads it and starts the game.

## Server update (Portainer)

1. *Stacks → cybergram → Editor*: use the new `tools/server/docker-compose.yml`.
   - Port `8080:8080/tcp` serves launcher updates.
   - The `cybergram-data:/data` volume holds player accounts.
   - Log rotation is included.
   - A `tls` folder holds the certificate for encrypted login.
2. *Update the stack* with *Re-pull image*.

## Known gaps

- Skills have Unlock, Boost and Ultimate ranks. Fork and Mastery talent nodes
  are not built yet.
- Whisperfang fires full-auto rather than in bursts, and Halo bolts hit instantly.
- No gamepad rebinding.
