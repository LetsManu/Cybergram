## 1. Phase 0 report (current state, 2026-10-07, main @ ea4ec2e)

### 1.1 Map architecture
- Every map is **generated by a builder script**, never hand-edited:
  - `tools/maps/build_shardline_front.gd` (2130 lines) writes `assets/maps/front/shardline_front.tscn`,
    `…_navmesh.res`, `…_navmesh_jungle.res`, `…_overview.tscn` and `assets/data/match/map_front.tres`.
  - `tools/maps/build_shardline_causeway.gd` builds the 1-lane slice (`map_slice_lane.tres`).
  - Post-pass: `tools/maps/level_garrison_posts.gd`.
  - **Rule for all chunks below: change the builder, re-run it, never edit the .tscn.**
- Geometry uses `tools/maps/kit/map_kit.gd`: StaticBody slabs, discs, wedges and trimesh rolling
  terrain. Walkable floors are registered as 2D shapes, and edge blockers and rails are generated (`_build_edges`).
- **Axes:** lanes run along -Z, GDD distance L maps to z = -L. Concord HQ back wall is at L 0, Syndicate at L 420,
  and half B mirrors half A across L 210. The playable box is about x -90..90 by z +6..-426.
- **Data:** `MapDef` holds lanes, hqs, mid_plaza, jungle_paths, jungle_pockets and water_zones
  (JUNGLE_NAV_LAYER 2). It is supported by `LaneDef`, `HardpointDef`, `FlankLoopDef`, `HqDef` and `JunglePocketDef`.
- **Queues:** normal and ranked 5v5 play `shardline_front`, ARAM plays `slice`.

### 1.2 Three lanes (exist)

| Lane | x | Main width | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| North | -80 | 20 m | "bridge lane": dip -2 m at L 100-130, bridge +3.5 m at L 160-196 |
| Center | 0 | 16 m | sunken market street L 100-131 (y -3.5), rises at L 40-70 and L 159-181 |
| South | +80 | 16 m | rise +2.5 m at L 160-196 |

- Side lanes leave the HQ through gates at x ±22, a stub to L 64, and lateral causeways to x ±90.
- **There is no lane polyline.** A lane is its 5 hardpoints (L 85/145/210/275/335). Width lives only
  in the builder constants. Side lanes and center are functionally similar today: same hardpoint
  rhythm, same task mix.

### 1.3 Jungle (geometry only)
- `_jungle` (builder :1477-1600) has 4 quadrants between the lanes (lateral offset 14..65 m):

  - its own nav region on layer 2, with 6 NavigationLink3D per quadrant to the lanes.
- 8 `JunglePocketDef` pockets, 14×14 m: `courtyard_*` at L 86 and `night_plaza_*` at L 129. They are
  reserved "for a neutral camp later".
- **No neutral camp, creep, timer or reward code.** `game-concept.md:222` still says "jungle camps cut
  until post-launch", and GDD `match-flow-and-map.md` §3.2 still says "between lanes impassable". Both
  are stale against W18-GEO.
- Related systems that already exist: Supply Cache (ammo refill, mana buff) and Garrison Sentinels.

### 1.6 Minimap limitations
`src/ui/hud/lane_minimap.gd` (318×204 px) draws **schematic lines from MapDef**: Sanctum→gate→hardpoint
chain, flank lines, faint jungle paths, hardpoint chips, own Armory/Foundry and the local hero arrow.
It has no floor shapes, causeways, plaza or jungle regions. It shows no allies, Wardlings, waves,
pings, reveal markers, last-seen markers, camps or timers. Bounds come only from Sanctums plus
hardpoints, and it has no zoom.

### 1.7 Navigation limitations
- The builders bake an engine navmesh: cell 0.25, agent radius 0.5, max climb 0.25, layer 1 lanes, layer 2 jungle.
- Bots (`bot_navigator.gd`) path on layers 1|2 and repath at 1 Hz. They recover from getting stuck by jumping or side-stepping.
- Lane choice is `bot_lane_planner.gd`. There is no lane waypoint graph.
- Wardlings path on **layer 1 only**, so they never enter the jungle.
- **No runtime navmesh change exists:** no NavigationObstacle3D carving, no region toggling.
  The dynamic corridor needs this.

### 1.8 Lane defenses
**No towers, turrets or inhibitors exist.** Defense is done by:
- Garrison Sentinels (2 per held hardpoint, 26 m range, respawn 45 s);
- Ward Generators (Breach);
- Uplink exposure gating;
- Barricades (sockets only).

### 1.9 Vision
**There is no fog of war, ward or sensor.** The server sends **every hero to every client every tick**
(`server_world._entities_for`). The anti-wallhack `InterestManager` designed in `architecture.md` §8.6 was
never built.

Only these reveal or check line of sight:
- **Reveals:**
  - `RevealSet` (abilities);
  - the Tracer ammo mod (1.5 s mark);
  - the REVEALED bit, used only for the through-wall outline.
- **Line-of-sight checks:**
  - nameplates;
  - bot perception (70 m sight).

## 3. Phased plan (owner priority A -> G -> E -> B, supporting work where needed)

Each phase lists its chunks. One chunk is one session-sized deliverable with its own commit and evidence.

### Phase A: map foundation
- **A1 Metrics and audit (docs + tool, no geometry).**
  - Write `tools/maps/map_metrics.gd`, a headless measurement tool. It loads `map_front`, measures navmesh path
    lengths and times (walk and sprint) for base→each lane T1/T2, base→jungle pockets, base→Mid,
    lane↔lane via jungle and via the Conduit, retreat paths, lane and path widths (raycast sweep), and the longest
    unbroken sightlines per area.
  - Write `docs/map-metrics.md` with the numbers from §1.10 plus the measured values, and `docs/map-audit.md`
    (dead ends, spawn-trap risks, sightlines over the cap, asymmetries, readability).
  - Tests: `tests/integration/map/route_metrics_test.gd` pins travel-time bounds and the symmetry between halves.
- **A2 Lane identity and Conduit skeleton (builder).**
  - *Center:* keep it short, with 2 Conduit mouths, and make it the rotation hub.
  - *Side lanes:* more isolated, with 1 shortcut each into the jungle and a longer rotation. Keep the North bridge
    identity, and give South its own (e.g. a flooded dock with a high gantry).
  - *Conduit:* promote the Undercroft + plaza spokes to "the Conduit".
    - Data: `MapDef.conduit` (`ConduitDef`: segments, mouths, widths).
    - Geometry: widen the strategic legs to about 8 m; keep at least 2 mouths per half.
    - Name it in the data, the minimap and the callouts.
  - Every geometry change goes through the builder, then re-bake, the placement validator and the edge-blocker
    test. Update GDD §3.2 and `game-concept.md` (jungle not impassable, camps un-cut).
- **A3 Route graph and validation.**
  - Add a `MapRouteGraph` built from MapDef: nodes are spawns, gates, hardpoints, pockets, Conduit mouths, caches
    and the Armory; edges are checked against the navmesh.
  - Add `tests/integration/map/route_connectivity_test.gd`: every node pair is reachable; no dead-end
    pocket without 2 exits; every spawn has 2 or more exits; retreat from every hardpoint to base stays under the
    bound; every Supply Cache can be reached.
  - Add a bot route sim: N bots walk all route pairs headless and log stuck positions to
    `production/qa/evidence/map-routes/`.

### Phase G: dynamic terrain (one event)
- **G1 MapEvent framework (server).**
  - `MapEventDef` data, plus `MapEventSystem` with the deterministic state machine
    STABLE -> WARNING -> TRANSITIONING -> COLLAPSED|OPENED -> STABLE_DYNAMIC. It is seeded by the match seed and
    runs on fixed match times.
  - **Replication:** a new map-state block in the snapshot (event id, state, state end tick) as a full-state blob
    that resyncs on reconnect. **This is a protocol bump (23).**
  - Tests cover every transition, the timing, determinism across seeds, and resync after reconnect.
- **G2 Collision and navigation switch.**
  - Pick one Conduit leg per half (e.g. `ToPlaza`).
  - **Collision:** the builder makes 2 variants of the leg: an open floor plus a collapse blocker body that starts
    disabled. The server toggles the blocker body.
  - **Navigation:** pre-baked nav regions for both states, switched with `NavigationServer3D.region_set_enabled`
    (no runtime bake). This is cheaper and deterministic.
  - **Player safety:** before COLLAPSED, the server pushes anyone inside the volume to the nearest safe exit
    (via the navmesh). This is never a kill.
  - **Bots and waves:** they repath on a state-change signal.
  - Tests: nobody is inside the volume after the change, and a bot reaches its target through the alternative route.
- **G3 Presentation.**
  - Warning lights, an animated shutter or rubble, a countdown on the HUD and in the world, an announcer line,
    3D audio and the minimap state.
  - Screenshots before, during and after, reviewed.

### Phase E: jungle role, camps, interactables
- **E1 Design (economy-designer + game-designer, GDD first).** Write `design/gdd/jungle-and-neutrals.md`:
  - **Roster** (original concepts):
    - Rogue Wardling pack: minor Lumen camp.
    - Security drone nest: weapon-cache camp with a temporary weapon buff.
    - Mana extractor: high risk, high reward.
    - Corrupted Conduit node: the epic neutral, in the Conduit or Mid.
  - Spawn and respawn timers, health, damage, aggro/leash, a clear-time target, Lumen, buffs and their durations,
    and death/reset rules.
  - Reward timing checked against the §18 Lumen curve with the balance-check skill (no early snowball).
  - Interactables: Lumen shards, a short speed pad, a scan beacon (information), an objective charge.
  - Update the registry.
- **E2 Neutral server systems.** Add `NeutralCampDef`, `NeutralSystem` (spawn, respawn, aggro, leash, reset,
  rewards) and `WeaponBuffDef` (reusing the stat modifiers from Armory v2, with a source per buff and timed expiry).
  Reward Lumen through `ProgressionSystem`. Tests cover each rule.
- **E3 Camp presentation and replication.** Models (from the Wardling pipeline + drones), telegraphs, audio,
  camp state and timers on the wire, and minimap icons.
- **E4 Jungle role.**
  - Add a `jungle` role tag.
  - Add a jungle guide to the BuildAdvisor (start recommendation) and a jungle panel: camp timers, income, buff
    status, a route suggestion, gank windows and objective prep.
  - Bots learn to clear camps. No hero is locked out.
- **E5 Epic neutral.** Arena, mechanics, team reward and announcer.

### Phase B: objective arenas
B1 Holdstone, B2 Mana Uplink, B3 Ward Generator, B4 Forward Beacon, B5 Barricades (gameplay finally),
B6 Sanctum + Foundry.

Each arena needs:
- 2 or more approaches (one safe, one risky), a flank and a retreat;
- cover sized from the metrics, sightlines at or under the cap, and a boundary;
- clearance for heroes, Wardlings and VFX;
- a minimap shape, state feedback, navmesh coverage and the placement validator.

B6 also implements the Sanctum heal / no-entry rule and the faction silhouettes.

## 4. Blocking questions (answer before the chunk that needs them)

1. **Conduit = Undercroft + plaza spokes?** (needed for A2). Recommended: yes. It reuses existing tunnels and links
   both side lanes to Mid, and the corridor event (G) uses one of its legs. Alternative: a new surface boulevard.
2. **Inhibitor-equivalent** (needed for D1). Recommended: a **Relay Spire** per lane just outside each HQ gate.
   When destroyed: that lane's HQ gate turret goes offline, the lane's Vanguard waves become "Overclocked"
   (stronger), and the enemy may counter-push to rebuild it after N minutes. Alternative: reuse Barricades
   as the lane gate.
3. **Lane defense tiers** (needed for D1). Recommended: no classic towers. Outer = Garrison Sentinels (exist),
   Inner = Ward Generator (exists), plus new HQ gate turrets and the Relay Spire. Alternative: add LoL-style
   towers on top (more work, and overlaps the Sentinels).
4. **"Sanctum and Foundry are both team bases"** (needed for B6). Today the Sanctum is the spawn/shop zone and the
   Foundry is the squad pad, **both inside each team's HQ**. Do you mean (a) Concord's base is called "Sanctum" and
   Syndicate's "Foundry", each with its own faction architecture, or (b) keep both as areas inside every base?
   Recommended: (a). The final HQ objective would then be the Uplink inside the Sanctum / Foundry.
5. **Vision server culling** (V1, protocol bump). Stopping wallhacks means the server stops sending unseen enemies.
   This is the biggest network change. Recommended: yes, bundled with the map-event protocol bump (23).

Defaults if unanswered: take every "Recommended" option.
