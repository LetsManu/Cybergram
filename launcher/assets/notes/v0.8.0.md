# Cybergram v0.8.0: polished client, lobby and launcher (pre-alpha)

The menus, the lobby and the launcher are redesigned around one shared look,
modelled on the League of Legends client. Cybergram keeps its own style and
uses no Riot assets. The network protocol is unchanged (14), so v0.7.0 servers
and clients stay compatible. Updating is still recommended.

## New main menu (client shell)

- **Top bar:**
  - A big glowing **PLAY** button. It opens a mode select (Online, Vs Bots,
    Practice Range, Tutorial, Movement Course); confirm with **CONFIRM**.
  - Tabs: **HOME / HEROES / PROFILE / SETTINGS**.
  - Your account chip with a status ring, plus settings and quit.
- **Hero showcase:** your selected hero in 3D on a slowly turning pedestal, with
  role and weapon. Switch heroes with the arrows or the badge strip.
- **"What's new"** and Practice / Tutorial tiles.
- **Friends sidebar:** grouped into Requests / Online / In lobby / In match /
  Offline / Pending. It folds into a slim icon strip.
- **Quit** asks for confirmation first.
- **Restyled screens:** login, profile, settings and pause use the same frames,
  tabs and buttons.

## New lobby (hero select)

- A phase title and countdown at the top: *Choose your hero → Lock in! →
  Match starting*.
- Your team on the left and the enemy team on the right, as large cards with
  hero, name, tag and status (Picking / Locked / Reconnecting). Open slots say
  "Bot will fill".
- In the centre, the selected hero in 3D with role, weapon and all four skills
  described. Below it, the hero grid with search and role filters.
- A big **LOCK IN** button. Each lock-in flashes the card and plays a lock
  sound, for other players too.
- Lobby chat in its own card, plus a short VS reveal of both teams before the
  match.

## New launcher

- A Riot Client-style layout:
  - a sidebar (Home / Patch notes / Settings)
  - animated key art
  - the latest release headline, with news cards and "Read more"
- The **PLAY** button changes between **Install / Update / Updating** (with the
  progress, size and speed inside the button) **/ Verifying / Repairing / Play**.
- Server status and your account at the top right. Login is a pop-up, with
  "Play as guest" on guest-only servers.
- The launcher now uses the game's colours, font and background. The shared
  files are checked by a test, so the two can't drift apart.

## Look and feel

- A new display font, **Orbitron** (SIL Open Font License; the licence is
  included).
- One palette for the game and the launcher: violet for actions, brass as the
  second accent, cyan for information.
- Short hover and press animations, and an animated skyline background.
- **Settings > Video > Reduce motion** turns all menu animations off.

## Known gaps

- At 1280×720 the 3D hero in the lobby is small. It looks best at 1080p.
- Gamepad navigation in the new screens uses the default focus order. It hasn't
  been tested with a real controller.
- The launcher's Windows build and gamepad navigation are untested.
