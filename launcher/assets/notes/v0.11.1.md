# Cybergram v0.11.1: Comfort and damage feedback (pre-alpha)

The network protocol is unchanged (15), so v0.11.0 servers and players still
work together. Updating is recommended.

## Comfort settings against motion sickness

A new **Comfort** tab in Settings.

- **Motion comfort preset:** one button sets every comfort option at once:
  - less camera recoil, no weapon bob, calmer effects
  - the centre dot, smooth corrections and the vignette
  - reduce motion, and a wider field of view

  The first time you start the game, a short hint points you to it.
- **Camera recoil (0–100%):** recoil is the same for everyone, so your shots
  land in the same place either way. Lower values keep the screen still and
  move the crosshair instead.
- **Weapon bob:** turn off the gun's walking sway.
- **Screen effects intensity (0–100%):** tones down hit flashes, muzzle light
  and other flashes. At 0% there are no full-screen flashes.
- **Field of view:** now goes up to 120°.
- **Fixed centre dot:** a small dot that always stays at the centre of the
  screen, as a steady point for your eyes. You can set its size and opacity.
- **Smooth corrections:** small corrections from the server now glide instead
  of snapping your view. Teleports and respawns still snap.
- **Comfort vignette:** optional soft darkening at the screen edges during
  fast moves like slides, dashes, knockbacks and falls. It is off by default.

## Damage feedback

- **Damage direction:** when another hero shoots you, a red arc around the
  crosshair points to where they are, including behind you. Bigger hits show
  a bigger arc. It follows your view as it fades.
- **Other damage:** damage from the world shows a ring instead of an arc. That
  covers falls, the Sudden Death ring and Leyfall, and for now also Wardlings
  and abilities.
- **Damage vignette:** a red edge flash when you are hit, stronger for bigger
  hits.
- **Calmer when you want it:** both follow Screen effects intensity. At 0%, or
  with Reduce motion, the arc stays visible as a plain fade with no pulsing,
  and the vignette is only a thin edge tint.

## Sudden Death you can see

- **The ring is visible now:** in Sudden Death, a glowing wall marks the
  shrinking ring in the Mid Plaza, and the ring also shows on the lane minimap.
- **Outside warning:** "OUTSIDE THE RING" appears while you're taking ring
  damage, and the damage arc points you back to safety.

## More fixes and additions

- **Damage direction works for every attacker:** heroes, Wardlings, bolts and
  abilities. The ring is only used for falls, Leyfall and the Sudden Death
  ring.
- **Dynamic crosshair:** your crosshair now opens up with your weapon's real
  bullet spread while you fire. You can turn this off in the Gameplay
  settings.
- **Colourblind mode** (F6) now also recolours the damage arc and vignette,
  and a small chevron shows the direction without relying on colour.
- **Dock water:** the water in the South lane docks now slows you (and
  Wardlings) by 15%, with a splash as you step in. Slows don't stack: the
  strongest slow wins.

## Known gaps

- **Turrets and structures** don't deal damage yet, so there's nothing to
  point at.
- **Distant instant abilities:** damage from an instant ability cast from very
  far away (more than 45 m) shows the ring instead of an arc.
