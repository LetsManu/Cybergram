# Cybergram v0.13.0: Matchmaking and a living map (pre-alpha)

The network protocol changes to 17. The server and every player must update.
The launcher updates itself to 1.3.0 the first time you open it.

## Matchmaking

**PLAY ONLINE** now opens matchmaking instead of the lobby.

- **Queues:**
  - **Normal 5v5** on Shardline Front. Empty seats are filled with labelled
    bots after about 75 seconds. Bot games are unrated.
  - **Ranked 5v5**, real players only, never bots.
  - **3v3 All Random**, the fun mode. Everyone gets a random hero, with
    rerolls, a shared bench and hero swaps between teammates.
  - **Custom game**, where the host picks the rules.
- **Lane preference:** pick a first and second lane (North, Center, South,
  Flex) or Fill. You see the estimated wait and how many are in the queue.
- **Ready check:** accept within 10 seconds. Declining or missing it gives a
  short queue lockout that grows if it keeps happening. Everyone else goes
  back to the front of the queue.
- **Hero draft (5v5):** teams pick in turns, 1-2-2-2-2-1. A hero can be on
  both teams but only once per team. If your timer runs out, a random hero is
  picked for you.
- **Ranks:** Ranked shows your rating number and a medal from Iron to Master
  after a few calibration games. Normal and 3v3 use a hidden rating of their
  own.
- **Fair play:**
  - **Reconnect:** if you drop, you can rejoin your running match. A bot plays
    your hero until you are back.
  - **Leavers:** leaving a match costs extra rating and a strike. Repeated
    strikes give a ranked lockout.
  - **Remake vote:** if a player never shows up or leaves in the first few
    minutes, the team can vote to end the game with no rating change.
  - **After the match:** honour a player, or report one by category.
- **Crashes:** if a match server crashes, the match is cancelled with no
  rating change and everyone is told.

## A living map

Shardline Front has been rebuilt.

- **Hills and levels:** the lanes now roll up and down by 2–3.5 m.
  - **North:** a raised bridge with guard towers, and a service walkway
    underneath.
  - **Center:** a sunken market street with terraces and shops you can enter.
  - **South:** docks with a warehouse, a loading ramp and a raised quay.
  - **Mid Plaza:** a bridge over the north spoke.
- **Jungle paths:** alleys, rooftops and a catwalk link the lanes. Moving
  between lanes this way takes about 9–12 seconds; the lane itself is still
  the fastest way forward. Bots use the jungle; Wardlings stay in the lanes.
  The minimap shows the jungle routes.
- **City life around the map:** traffic, drones and a sky train in the
  distance, neon signs and holograms, fog, rain, steam vents and a city hum.
  The time of day and weather are the same for everyone in a match.
  - It all stays outside the play area and never crosses a lane sightline.
  - The new **World ambience** setting turns it down or off. Reduce motion
    stops flickering and slows the steam.

## For server hosts

The server now runs as a **front**: it handles accounts, friends, parties
and matchmaking, and starts a separate match process for each game. A
crashing match no longer takes the other matches down.

- **Forward UDP 7800–7809** in addition to UDP 7777 and TCP 8080.
- **Create a `.env` file** from `tools/server/.env.example`.
- **Set a ticket key:** `CYBERGRAM_TICKET_KEYS=k1:` followed by
  `openssl rand -hex 32`.
- **Auto-update (optional):** a timer checks for a new server image every
  12 minutes and restarts only when no match is running (at most 30 minutes
  later). Pin `CYBERGRAM_IMAGE_TAG` to stay on a version.
- Step-by-step guide: `docs/HOSTING.md`, "Upgrading to v0.13". To go back to
  the old single-process server, set `CYBERGRAM_MODE=single`.

## Privacy

`PRIVACY.md` is now version 4. It covers ratings, match history, strikes,
reports and honour.

- **Match history:** only ids, heroes, result and duration, deleted after
  180 days.
- **Reports:** categories only, deleted after 30 days.
- **Strikes:** deleted at most 30 days after the last one.
- **Account deletion:** removes all of the above.
- **Guests:** cannot play Ranked, and their ratings are never stored.

## Known gaps

- **Party lanes:** in a party, only the leader's lane choice counts; the
  others queue as Fill.
- **Bot heroes:** bots that fill a seat play their own hero, not one drafted
  for that seat.
- **Match history:** "Recent matches" in your profile stays empty for now.
- **Post-match stats:** only kills, deaths and assists are shown.
- **Old lobby:** set `CYBERGRAM_MATCHMAKING=off` to use it instead of
  matchmaking.
- **Server cost:** the new map costs the server about 20% more time per
  update. That is still well inside the budget.
- **Untested on real graphics hardware:** lighting inside buildings and the
  cost of the city layer.
