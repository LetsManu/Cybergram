# Cybergram v0.19.0: The new Armory and a new look (pre-alpha)

**The network protocol goes from 20 to 22: everyone must update**, players and
servers alike. An old client cannot join a new server. The launcher updates to
1.9.0 (same protocol) and fetches the game for you.

The Armory is rebuilt around **recipes**, and the gear you buy is now visible on
your hero and your gun.

## New: recipes

- Small **Components** (250 to 400 Lumen) combine into **Assemblies** (about 800
  to 1,000) and then into **Signatures** (about 2,400 to 2,550).
- When you buy an item, the parts you already own are used up and you pay only
  for the missing parts plus a small combine cost. The shop shows the recipe
  tree with your parts ticked.
- You can own **two Signatures** and **one copy of any item**. Each Signature
  has its own named passive: **14 Signature passives** (8 on the gun, 6 on the
  body).

## New: gun sockets and six slots

- Your gun has **Core, Barrel and Frame sockets** and a **Chamber** for one Ammo
  Type and one Ammo Mod. Crystals on Mana guns, Chips on Mechanical guns.
- Your hero has **6 open item slots** for components, body gear and Signatures.
- **All six Ammo Types and all five Ammo Mods** are on sale, with their tracer
  colours and impact effects.
- New body gear for health, **armor** (against weapon damage) and **resist**
  (against skill damage).

## New: gear you can see

- What you buy shows on your gun and on your hero: belt, chest, shoulders, back,
  head and legs. Other players' builds are visible too, so you can read an
  enemy's loadout at a glance.

## New: the shop

- A new full-screen shop with three tabs: **Recommended**, **All Items** and
  **Item Sets**.
- **Recommended** shows your next purchase and why, your build path with
  choices, and situational counters when the match calls for them.
- **All Items** has stat filters, the recipe tree and "builds into".
- A bottom strip always shows your Lumen, sockets, slots and a one-press
  **Undo last**. Anything bought during a visit can be undone for a full refund.

## New: Item Sets

- Item Sets are the old My builds, renamed: your own builds of finished items
  for each hero. Copy one as a text string and paste it for a friend. Old
  builds are kept but marked as made for the old Armory.

## New: lighting, shadows and shaders

- A new default look: a warm **golden-hour sun** with long shadows, cool
  violet shade, and brighter **neon** on signs, trims and the Uplink core.
- **Shadows** from the sun on every quality level from Medium up (sharper
  cascades on High and Ultra), lamps that cast shadows (2 / 4 / 8 by quality)
  and simple shadows under heroes and Wardlings on Low.
- Map surfaces get more detail: worn edges on walls, a highlight band, grime
  where walls meet the floor and better contact shading.
- The old look is still there with the `--look current` launch option.

## Changed

- Bots buy the new items too: they build recipes part by part and sell a loose
  item to make room.
- The Lumen sound effect was removed.

## Hero changes

Every hero has a new guide (a Med-Pack first, then recipe steps, squad upgrades
and counters). The old Mount tiers (Ember Heart I to III and so on) are gone:
each line is now a recipe.

### Vesper
- Commander guide: squad upgrades first, Squad Expansion I before the first
  Signature.

### Ryker
- Gun-first guide: Ember Heart early, then Bore Rail and a body Signature.

### Brannoc
- Bulwark Gunner guide: Reinforced Cores, then Bastion Plate or Anchor Frame.
