# Cybergram v0.18.1: crash fix, Garrison Sentinels and Supply Caches (pre-alpha)

The network protocol stays at 20. The launcher stays at 1.8.0 and fetches the
new game build on its own.

## Fixed

- **The game lagged more and more and then crashed after a few minutes in a
  match.** When a snapshot was full, a newly spawned Wardling could miss one
  update; the game then deleted and rebuilt its animated model, again and
  again, until it ran out of room and crashed. Wardling models now stay until
  the server says the Wardling is gone.

## New: Garrison Sentinels

- Every held hardpoint is guarded by **two Sentinels**: plated Wardlings on
  glowing sockets. They shoot enemy heroes and Wardlings in range (26 m) and
  never leave the zone.
- A new owner's Sentinels arrive 10 s after a capture; the old owner's dissolve.
  A fallen Sentinel returns after 45 s (its socket glows dim meanwhile).
- They get stronger with each Surge, and pay 1.5 times a squad Wardling's
  bounty (half for repeat kills at the same hardpoint within 3 minutes).

## New: Supply Caches

- Each held hardpoint has an **ammo crate** for its owners (from 10 s after a
  capture; its lights blink while it switches sides).
- **Mechanical** guns refill 25 % of their reserve per second within 3 m.
- **Mana** guns: touch it and your mana starts to recover twice as fast for
  10 s (once every 8 s).
