# Cybergram v0.17.0: Queue, party and hero select like a real client (pre-alpha)

The network protocol changes to 20. The server and every player must update.
The launcher updates itself to 1.7.0 the first time you open it.

## Queue and status

- **Status bar** at the bottom of the play screens: connection and ping, what
  the server thinks you are doing, the queue timer with the expected wait,
  your lockout countdown, and a plain hint when a wait runs long.
- **Diagnostics.** A button opens the last network events with a Copy button,
  for bug reports. It never contains your password or match tickets.
- **No more stuck states.** Logging in and queueing at the same moment no
  longer leaves you shown as Offline; after a reconnect the client asks the
  server for its real state.

## Party and friends

- **Friends list with 7 states:** Online, Away, In lobby, In queue, In hero
  select, In match and Offline, with the game mode and each its own shape.
- **Messages** to online friends, with unread counts.
- **Party tools:** Ready, Leave, make someone Leader, Kick, and party chat on
  the play screen.
- **Ask to join** a friend's party; their leader gets the request.
- **Invites show a countdown** and disappear when they run out, in the game
  and in the launcher.
- **Away** after 5 minutes without input.
- **Fixed:** your party was lost about a minute into a match; the play screen
  did not show your real party online; invites from someone who left still
  worked.

## Hero select

- **Hover:** click a hero to show your team what you want. A hovered hero is
  locked in for you when your time runs out.
- **Ranked:** if you have nothing hovered when time runs out, hero select is
  cancelled and it counts as a dodge (lockout and rating penalty).
- **Normal is blind pick:** you see enemy heroes only when everyone has locked.
- **Trades:** 20 seconds at the end to swap heroes with a teammate.
- **Team chat** in hero select, only to your team.
- **Ability preview:** the four abilities with their keys under the hero name.
- **Ready check** now 12 seconds. Keyboard and controller work: arrows to
  choose, Enter to lock in, Esc to leave (it asks first).

## Custom games and loading

- **Bots per team** (0-5 or fill) and **bot difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard).
- The host can change map, mode and size after opening the lobby.
- **Loading screen shows every player's progress.** The game starts when your
  own map is loaded, not after a fixed wait.

## Sound

- Matchmaking now has its sounds: match found, your turn, the last seconds,
  victory and defeat.

## For server hosts

- **Update the server image to v0.17.0** (protocol 20).
- **Health and metrics** on TCP 8090 (published on 127.0.0.1): `/health`,
  `/metrics` for Prometheus or Icinga. See `docs/monitoring.md`.
- **Admin page** `/admin`: set `CYBERGRAM_ADMIN_TOKEN` (a long random string)
  in `.env` next to the compose file. Without it `/admin` stays closed.
- **Logs are JSON lines** with salted player tags, never names or account ids.

## Privacy

`PRIVACY.md` is now version 7: party chat, messages and hero select chat are
only passed on while you are online, never stored and never logged. Your
loading progress is shown to the players of your match.

## Known gaps

- Bans are built in but set to 0 until the roster has more heroes.
- Checked on a virtual display only so far: two-player flows (invites, chat,
  trades) still need a check on real machines (`docs/manual-checklist.md`).
